Study Guide

Olympiad-level STEM requires more than just memorizing formulas. Here are the core heuristics and techniques for building true visual intuition.

The Feynman Technique

If you can't explain a concept in simple terms, you don't understand it. After watching a derivation, close the PDF and try to explain the mechanism aloud from scratch.

Active Recall

Re-reading textbooks creates an illusion of competence. Force your brain to retrieve information. Use blank sheets of paper to derive formulas without looking at the source.

Visual Mapping

Never accept an algebraic equation without mapping it to a physical geometry. Ask yourself: "What does this variable physically stretch, compress, or rotate?"

Pro Tip for Competitions

When doing practice sets, spend 80% of your time on the problems you got wrong. Identify exactly which heuristic or logical leap you missed, and write it down.
