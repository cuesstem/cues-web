About Cues

STEM subjects are often taught as a collection of formulas to be memorized. We built Cues because we believe math and science should be visualized.

By combining programmatic animations (via tools like Manim) with rigorous, competition-level problem-solving, we aim to bridge the gap between abstract equations and physical intuition.
